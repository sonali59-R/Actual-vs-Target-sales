MIS Sales Reporting Dashboard

The company wanted to track sales performance across multiple business units and compare actual sales against targets, so they could quickly identify any gaps.

My responsibility was to consolidate all raw sales data into a single dashboard and calculate actual vs target performance, including variance percentages.



Built an Actual vs Target Sales MIS dashboard tracking ₹46.5L+ actual sales against ₹46.4L targets, with automated variance and variance % calculations.

Monitored monthly and YTD performance across 4 business units (Delish, Juicies, Tempo, Yummies), improving visibility into team-wise sales performance.

Identified a 0.1% overall variance between actual and target sales and highlighted month-level deviations using conditional formatting.

Automated tracking of monthly target achievement counts (7+ targets), enabling quick identification of top- and under-performing sales periods.

Created employee-level performance reports covering 20+ sales representatives, showing individual monthly target attainment.

Designed 12-month sales trend analysis, comparing actual vs target values to detect seasonal patterns and performance gaps.

Enabled dynamic filtering by year and team, reducing manual reporting effort and improving MIS turnaround time.

Consolidated raw sales data into a single MIS dashboard, supporting faster management review and decision-making.
